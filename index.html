A few clicks away

Less setup. More listening.

Here’s how to get your app up and running.

01 / WINDOWS

Open it and make yourself at home.

Download VibeDrop.exe and double-click to launch. After the first run, find VibeDrop in your Windows Start menu.

02 / ANDROID

Your library, in your pocket.

Open VibeDrop.apk and tap Install. If prompted, allow installs from your browser or file manager. Install updates over the existing app to keep your songs.

03 / VIDEO TOOLS

Extract, launch, and download.

For YouTube and TikTok, download the ZIP, right-click and choose Extract All, then open the included EXE. Keep the app running until downloads finish.

A little better with every update.

VibeDrop checks for updates while running. You can also check any time in Settings → App updates, then choose when to download and install.

Good to know

A few quick answers.

The essentials before you press play.

Can I listen without internet?

Yes. Songs you have downloaded in VibeDrop can play offline. Searching, discovering new music, downloading songs, and accessing cloud backup need an internet connection.

Do I need to extract VibeDrop from a ZIP?

No. The Windows download is a single EXE. The Android download is an APK. Only the separate YouTube and TikTok downloaders come in ZIP files.

How do I keep my library when updating?

Use Settings → App updates in VibeDrop. On Android, install the new APK over your existing app rather than uninstalling it. On Windows, keep your existing library folder when replacing the app.

Where do the downloads come from?

VibeDrop is hosted on our GitHub releases. The separate YouTube and TikTok downloads use the original MediaFire links.
