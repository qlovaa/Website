The Voyage

There is always
a horizon ahead.

CharterShip is built around a shared voyage: landfall, gathering, crafting, fishing, sailing, discovery and a seven-stage Captain's Journal leading toward the Last Anchorage. Explore freely when the story is complete.

On the water

Made for a crew.

A self-contained world with procedural islands, an upgradeable crew ship, survival systems and cooperative multiplayer for up to eight players.
